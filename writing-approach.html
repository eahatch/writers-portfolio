Working thesis

Professional and technical writing is a human practice situated within complex social and technological networks. Although institutions, tools, and conventions shape communication, writers exercise judgment in interpreting situations, imagining audiences, and making information usable. Its value depends on how responsibly those choices serve the people affected by a document.
